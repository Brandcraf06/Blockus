### Blockus 2.17.4
* Added Frozen Magma Block, which behaves like Magma Block but inflicts freezing damage
  * Mobs wearing leather armor or immune to freezing are immune
  * Mobs vulnerable to freezing (Blaze, Magma Cube, Strider) take additional damages
  * Crafted using 1 Magma Block and 1 Blue Ice
* Added Packed Ice Brick Stairs/Slab & Chiseled Packed Ice
* Renamed Ice Bricks to Packed Ice Bricks, Ice Brick Wall to Packed Ice Brick Wall and Ice Pillar to Packed Ice Pillar
* Backported Packed Ice & Snow Bricks textures from Minecraft 26.4
* Changed Packed Ice & Snow Pillars textures
* Packed Ice Bricks can now be made only using Packed Ice
* Packed Ice Bricks/Pillar now requires a pickaxe to be obtained
* Packed Ice Bricks/Pillar no longer requires Silk Touch to be obtained
* Snow Bricks/Pillar now requires a shovel to be obtained
* Snow Bricks blocks can no longer be made using stonecutter
* Removed the `#blockus:natural_ice` item tag
* Added Crying Obsidian Bricks (Stairs/Slab/Wall), Cracked Crying Obsidian Bricks, Small Crying Obsidian Bricks (Stairs/Slab/Wall), Crying Obsidian Pillar, Crying Obsidian Circular Paving, Crying Obsidian Door & Crying Obsidian Trapdoor
  * Unlike regular Crying Obsidian, these blocks don't emit particles
* Updated the textures of all Blockus obsidian blocks
* Renamed Obsidian Reinforced Door to Obsidian Door
* Renamed Obsidian Reinforced Trapdoor to Obsidian Trapdoor
* Added Mossy Cobbled Deepslate (Stairs/Slab/Wall) & Mossy Cobbled Deepslate Bricks (Stairs/Slab/Wall)
* Food crates and bread boxes can no longer be composted
* Hedges no longer "connect" to bars, panes and lattices
